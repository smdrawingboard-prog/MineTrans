The headline finding

Your largest exposure isn't the asset. It's the interruption.

Across two decades and 135 significant insured mining claims totalling US$15.3 billion, business interruption — not physical damage — represents approximately 80% of total gross losses. And the sector recovers a smaller share of its losses through insurance than almost any other industry.

The window to act is now. The property and business interruption market has softened, with double-digit rate reductions and roughly US$1.5 billion of capacity available per single mining risk — and South Africa named as a softening hotspot. Well-presented risk earns better terms.

The declared-value problem

Business interruption declared values are frequently wrong in both directions. Over-declare and you overpay premium every year.
