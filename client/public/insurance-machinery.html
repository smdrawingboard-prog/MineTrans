Insurance for Yellow Metal & Equipment

Comprehensive coverage for the various yellow metal and machinery that form the backbone of mining operations.

Equipment Coverage

Our machinery insurance covers:

  • Excavators and loaders
  • Haul trucks and dump trucks
  • Drilling equipment
  • Processing plant machinery
  • Conveyor systems
  • Crushers and mills
  • Flotation cells
  • Pumping and water systems
  • Electrical distribution equipment
  • Mobile support equipment

Coverage Features

  • All-risk protection against accidental damage
  • Breakdown and mechanical failure coverage
  • Replacement cost basis
  • Agreed value options for critical equipment
  • Spare parts and auxiliary equipment included
  • Coverage during transit and relocation

Valuation Methodology

Equipment is valued based on:

  • Age and condition assessments
  • Market comparable analysis
  • Replacement cost estimates
  • Depreciation schedules
  • Manufacturer specifications

Claims Settlement

Fast, efficient claims handling with:

  • On-site damage assessment
  • Repair vs. replacement evaluation
  • Direct repair arrangements with vendors
  • Expedited payment processing
  • Replacement equipment sourcing assistance

Extensions Available

  • Increased limits for high-value assets
  • Reduced excess options
  • Hire equipment coverage during repairs
  • Debris removal costs
  • Temporary repairs and emergency measures
  • Preventive maintenance coverage
