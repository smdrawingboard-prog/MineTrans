Why This Matters

Your Largest Exposure Isn't the Asset

Most MineTrans clients operate yellow metal. Excavators, haul trucks, drill rigs, and processing equipment are typically the largest share of the risk we underwrite—when that equipment fails, production stops.

For mining and processing plant operations, business interruption can be the largest single exposure. The mine stops earning long before the asset is rebuilt—though this applies to a smaller slice of our client base than machinery risk does.

Transit risk is often overlooked. High-value ore concentrates, doré bars, and equipment move through complex, remote supply chains. Each handover point is an exposure boundary.
