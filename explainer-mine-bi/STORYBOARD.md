# How a mine is insured: Business Interruption (90 s) — timed storyboard

Audience: mining CFO. One isometric cutaway, one continuous space, camera travels power line -> single chip.
Colour grammar: grey/pale = mine stopped · teal = covered/locked · vermilion = loss/damage · yellow = electricity.
Rule: <= 7 words on screen at once, each word held >= 0.3 s, one thing moves at a time.
