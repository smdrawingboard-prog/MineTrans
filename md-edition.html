The operational reality

One asset fails. Everything downstream stops.

A primary mill, a winder, a crusher, a key substation. Long lead-time equipment means an outage measured in months, not weeks — and across the sector, fires, explosions, machinery breakdown and geotechnical failures account for roughly 70% of total gross claim value, with the average fire and explosion claim at US$115 million.

Source: Marsh, Mining Losses Uncovered, Nov 2025.

03 — Financial Resilience

While you rebuild, the cash keeps flowing.

Properly structured business interruption cover pays your fixed costs, debt service and payroll for the full duration of the rebuild — so you're not choosing between people and creditors while the mill is down. We stress-test the indemnity period against real equipment lead times, not optimistic ones.

And the timing is on your side: the mining insurance market has softened, with double-digit rate reductions and around US$1.5 billion of capacity per risk. Operations that can evidence their risk management are commanding the best terms in years.
