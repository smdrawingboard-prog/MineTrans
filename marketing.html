The Criticalities one-pager.

The exposures that decide whether a mine keeps earning through a major loss. Use this as the agenda for your next risk review — with your engineering team in the room.

C·01

Business interruption

Roughly 80% of sector gross losses. Declared values must be stress-tested against real equipment lead times, not optimistic ones.

C·02

Critical assets

Mills, winders, crushers, substations and TSFs ranked by lead time and revenue dependency. If it stops the plant, it's critical.

C·03

Single points of failure

Any component with no redundancy. These convert one breakdown into a site-wide stoppage.

C·04

Fire & explosion

The sector's largest peril by claim value. Conveyor and plant fires drive the severity tail.

C·05

Tailings & geotechnical

GISTM alignment is a baseline underwriting expectation. Evidence of it earns terms.

C·06

Power & water

Systemic, not incidental. Both are primary BI triggers across the region — and both are mitigable.

C·07

Logistics & marine transit

Concentrate and equipment in transit — road, rail, port and sea, across multiple jurisdictions.

C·08

SASRIA & social risk

Strike, riot and civil commotion. We apply SASRIA extensions proactively to close gaps others miss.

C·09

Maintenance evidence

Condition monitoring, preventive maintenance and critical spares — your strongest card at renewal.
